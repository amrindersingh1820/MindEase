Tips for Good Sleep

Why Sleep Matters

Good sleep is essential for mental and physical health. It helps improve mood, focus, and overall well-being.

Tips for Better Sleep

  • Maintain a consistent sleep schedule.
  • Create a relaxing bedtime routine.
  • Limit screen time before bed.
  • Ensure your bedroom is dark, quiet, and cool.
  • Avoid caffeine and heavy meals in the evening.
  • Engage in regular physical activity.
  • Manage stress through relaxation techniques.

Disclaimer: These tips are for informational purposes only and are not a substitute for professional advice. If you have persistent sleep issues, consult a healthcare provider.
