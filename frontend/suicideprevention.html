Suicide Prevention

Recognizing the Signs

It's important to recognize the warning signs of suicide, such as withdrawal, hopelessness, or talking about wanting to die. If you notice these signs in yourself or others, take them seriously.

How to Help

  • Listen without judgment.
  • Encourage seeking professional help.
  • Stay connected and check in regularly.
  • Provide resources and support.

Resources

If you or someone you know is in crisis, please contact a suicide prevention hotline in your area. In the US, call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
